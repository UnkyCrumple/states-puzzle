Field notes

Place a tile to add a field note with its history.

How to play

  1. The labeled tiles wait on the shelf beside the map. Pick one up and it grows to full map size.
  2. Drop it where it belongs. A correct drop snaps into place and adds a field note. A wrong drop sends it back to the shelf.
  3. On a touch screen or keyboard, tap or press Enter on a tile to select it, then tap the map. Hint and Place it for me work on the selected tile.
  4. Score: 100 per tile, minus 10 per miss and 20 per hint. Stars reward tiles placed on the first try.
  5. Colors show when each state joined the Union. Grey pieces and the faint land around the edges are context, not tiles.

Sources and notes

    About the map data

    Borders come from a 2012 collection of historical US map files and were simplified for play, so edges are approximate. The repository does not state a license. Colonial charters and Native nations' lands are not drawn. For classroom use where borders must be exact, check them against the Newberry Library Atlas of Historical County Boundaries.
